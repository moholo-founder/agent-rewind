An AI safety director told her agent: "don't act until I tell you to."

Then she watched it start deleting her entire inbox — and she couldn't stop it from her phone. "I had to RUN to my Mac mini like I was defusing a bomb."

The cause of that OpenClaw incident? Context compaction. The long-running task pushed her instruction out of the agent's memory. The only stop button lived inside the agent's head — and the agent forgot it.

So I built the two things that were missing: a black box and an undo button.

Agent Rewind is a flight recorder for AI agents. It sits between the agent and its tools, records every action, snapshots state before anything destructive runs, and holds oversized operations for human approval.

In the video: a rogue agent wipes a 200-message inbox and deletes 40 quarterly reports. The operator clicks Rewind. Everything comes back.

⏺ Every action journaled — append-only, tamper-resistant
↩️ One-click undo — byte-identical restore from pre-action snapshots
🛑 A kill switch that lives OUTSIDE the agent's context — compaction can't erase it, and no amount of "creative reasoning" clears it

Works with any MCP tool stack (Claude Code, Claude Desktop, Cursor). Source-available, free for individuals and small teams, installs in seconds.
